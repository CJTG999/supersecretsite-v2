How to test offline:
1. Cache a video using the button above.
2. Open your browser's Developer Tools (F12 or Cmd+Option+I).
3. Go to the "Application" tab.
4. In the left sidebar, under "Service Workers", check the "Offline" checkbox.
5. Try playing the video again using the "Play Cached Video" button.
6. You can also disable your computer's Wi-Fi/network to test.
